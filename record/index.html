Method

Forecasts are generated by automated strategies from public Kalshi and Polymarket data. Every call is published with a receipt hash before the outcome is known and graded automatically at resolution with the Brier score against the market's own price. Impersonal, standardized output; not individualized advice.

Skill = market Brier − model Brier; positive means the model's probability was closer to the outcome than the market price was. ROI is the gross return in cents per $1 contract had one bought the model's side at the market price, minus a 1c fee estimate. Nothing here is a recommendation to any individual.
